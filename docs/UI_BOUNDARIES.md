# UI boundaries
/core is the independent operator surface: providers, diagnostics, work records, evidence, health and governance. /triad owns autonomous evolution views. /p2p is a separate human-directed product shell. Shared renderers do not imply shared product navigation or data.

Generic research-engine inspection is available from Core; product-specific research workflows are not introduced here. Legacy routes remain compatibility entries and clearly identify their owning surface.

Product servers must be independently stoppable. A missing product must not prevent Core startup or use of its kill switch. UI availability is not permission to execute work.
