# Core contract
Core owns execution mechanisms, providers, credentials, immutable assignments, audit, approval enforcement, persistence, provenance, data and research engines. Products choose objectives and workflows. Core never imports product packages or the legacy triad compatibility package.

Global authority cannot be increased by product policy. Existing no-live-capital, no-arbitrary-shell and bounded-write rules remain in force. Credential target names and existing record IDs are compatibility identifiers, not product ownership.

Product scopes are host-issued. Operator views may inspect all scopes. Product views only see their own records and explicitly shared data. Product scope must propagate through context, evidence, artifacts and graph traversal. A product cannot self-declare a record shared.

Core starts independently of either product. Product failure is reported as unavailable while Core governance remains accessible.
