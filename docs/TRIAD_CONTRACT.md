# Triad contract
Triad owns autonomous product evolution, its mission, improvement backlog, creation/review rotation, scheduled cycles and product UI. It may eventually engineer a complete trading product. This statement does not expand current capability grants.

Triad depends on Core mechanisms; it never imports P2P. It receives no curated P2P strategy knowledge. Core files, global governance and credentials remain protected. Product changes remain bounded and independently reviewed under the existing rules.
